Sizing

What we need to size your pump.

Don't have every number? Send what you have. Site photos and the nameplate of an existing pump help a lot.

  1. 01

    Application

    What the water is for: a villa, a building, a farm, a site or an industrial process.

  2. 02

    Flow rate

    How much water you need, for example in litres per minute or cubic metres per hour.

  3. 03

    Head & pressure

    How high and how far the water must travel, and the pressure needed at the outlets.

  4. 04

    Water source

    Mains, a rooftop or underground tank, or a well or borehole (with its depth).

  5. 05

    Power supply

    Single-phase, three-phase, generator or solar.

Which water pump do I need for my villa?

Most villas use a booster (pressure) pump fed from the water tank, so every tap and shower gets steady pressure. The right size depends on the number of floors and bathrooms and how many outlets run at once. Send us those details and we'll recommend a suitable pump set.

Why is water pressure low on upper floors, and can a pump fix it?

Low pressure on upper floors usually means the supply or tank can't deliver enough head to reach them. A correctly sized booster pump set raises and stabilises the pressure. We check the building height, pipe runs and demand before recommending one.

What is the difference between a submersible pump and a surface pump?

A submersible pump sits under water, inside the well, borehole or tank, and pushes water up. A surface pump, such as a centrifugal or booster pump, sits outside the water and draws it in, which only works over a short suction lift. Deep wells and boreholes need submersible pumps; surface pumps suit tanks, transfer and pressure duty.

Do solar water pumps work without the grid?

Yes. A solar pump runs directly from solar panels through a controller, so it needs no grid connection or diesel. It pumps during daylight, and a storage tank keeps water available at night. Systems can also be designed with grid or generator backup.

Do you supply and install water pumps in Sharjah and Ajman?

Yes. From our Dubai head office we supply, install and support pump systems in Dubai, Sharjah, Ajman and the other Emirates: Abu Dhabi, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah.

What information do you need to quote for a pump?

The application, the flow rate you need, the height and distance the water must travel (head), the water source and the available power supply. Photos of the site and of any existing pump's nameplate also help.
